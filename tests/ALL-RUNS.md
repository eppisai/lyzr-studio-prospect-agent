# All recorded test runs

Thirty-one runs in Lyzr Studio, from 3 to 5 October 2026, across six versions of the prompts. Twelve of them did not pass, all before the final version. The tables say what each one showed.

Full step-by-step records are published for the last four runs, in [transcripts](transcripts). The earlier runs are listed here from my working notes, with what each one showed.

## Version 1: a manager and three specialists, selling Lyzr's RFQ product to manufacturers (3 October)

| # | Account | Result | What it showed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Excel Conveyors | Failed | The manager called the analyst without passing on the research. Agents share no memory. |
| 2 | Excel Conveyors | Failed | The researcher answered the user directly and ended the run. |
| 3 | Excel Conveyors | Failed | The analyst wanted proof of pain before allowing any outreach. Too strict. |
| 4 | Zoho | Failed | It treated software that Zoho sells as Zoho's own factory. |
| 5 | Excel Conveyors | Failed | The writer returned the wrong thing, and the manager wrote the email itself. |
| 6 | Excel Conveyors | Failed | The researcher answered directly again. |
| 7 | Excel Conveyors | Passed | Research, analysis and the writer's drafts in about 34 seconds. |
| 8 | Zoho | Passed | "Not fit", and the writer was not called. |
| 9 | Apex Industrial | Passed | Unclear identity, so "needs research" and no draft. |
